PHASE 7 · CONNECTED MODE · TURN 1 The monitor drives; the phone explains Interstitial state machine, then three panes in both orientations, then the mid-session states. Frames are built to the UI-fix control spec: five button levels, accent means only four things, ≥44px targets, small mono labels never lighter than #57544c. Landscape frames are 844×390 and never scroll.
DECISION · ACTUAL VS TARGET The target is ink in connected mode — the actual beside it is the only coloured value, and its colour is the judgement: teal #2a6275 under target, ink on target, ochre #8a5f18 over. Beneath it sits a fixed TARGET 22 caption that never re-words — no fills, borders, arrows or changing text. Accent's only job on these panes is the active interval's countdown.
DECISION · PORTRAIT GRID Two-line rows. Line 1 carries Time · Meters · /500m at mono 15px; line 2 carries SPM · HR · Rest at mono 12px with their own labels. Six columns, always visible, no horizontal scroll.
DECISION · PANES Order A timer · B live · C grid. Lands on B, then remembers the last pane per rower. Dots sit bottom-centre in portrait, right-edge vertical in landscape, 44px targets, labelled.
DECISION · DISTANCE INTERVALS The programmed dimension is the one that counts down and the one that wears accent: time intervals count time down and meters up; distance intervals count meters down and time up.

Teal #2a6275 under, ink on, ochre #8a5f18 over — on the value itself. The caption underneath is a fixed TARGET 22 that never re-words, so nothing on the card moves or reflows while you row; the only thing that changes is the colour of a number you were already watching. The split obeys it identically — pane A's NOW · /500M card, pane B's hero numeral in both orientations, and the grid's actual /500M and SPM cells. Both hexes are the app's own AT and O2 type colours, and both clear AA on page and surface.
Pane B is one number tall enough to read from the doorway (96px portrait, tenths at 52px so the eye lands on the seconds), then time left, then a three-card row. It carries the timer's interval segment bar and its UP NEXT strip verbatim, so swiping between A and B never loses your place in the session. Nothing here is a control. Pane C two-line rows: 15px numbers on line 1, 12px on line 2, indented 30px under the interval number so the eye can still scan a column. The active row is a 2px ink card with a filled now-marker; its countdown is accent because it is a duration. Completed rows are actuals in ink over a solid rule; upcoming rows are programmed values in #57544c over a dashed rule — the app's existing "nothing here yet" idiom does the work that a lighter grey used to, and every value clears AA. Six columns, none hidden, no horizontal scroll. Distance intervals (row 5): the meters cell carries the programmed number and counts down when active; the time cell shows — until it accrues. A mono caption under the grid names it in plain words rather than inventing a glyph. Dots are a 56px band: 9px square, mono label, whole third is the tap target — 130×56 each. Labels stay because three unlabelled dots don't say what's behind them, and a wet thumb shouldn't have to explore.

DOTS IN LANDSCAPE A 56px rail on the right edge, three 56×56 targets, three-letter mono labels. Right edge because a mount's cradle arms crop bottom-centre, and because the swipe gesture (anywhere on the surface, 60px threshold) is the real navigation — the rail is confirmation, not the only route.
NO SCROLL, BY CONSTRUCTION Panes A and B are two fixed columns; nothing in them can grow. Pane C is the one exception and it is contained: the header, caption row and End button are pinned, and only the rows between them scroll — five fit at 390px, the active row is always scrolled to.
GLOVED HANDS One control per pane (End session), never at an edge a palm rests on. End is staged: it becomes Tap again to end for 4 s, so a stray touch mid-piece can't finish the session.
