Correction to the first version of this page

James caught it: the first draft drew both buttons as neutral outlines. They are not. The primary action is the house's level-one button, accent red with light text at 56px, and it is the same class on both the free-row door and the workout interstitial today. The frames above now lift the real classes rather than approximating them.

Two things to decide

1. The words. "Starting your row" over "STARTING THE ROW". The workout says "Sending the workout" over "SENDING THE WORKOUT", and a free row sends no workout, so the word cannot be reused. The alternative closest to the machine is "Sending the program".

2. What happens when the monitor never answers. The send gives up after five seconds. The card can either fall through to Ready anyway, letting the rower start the row on the PM5 itself, or show the failure card a workout shows, which offers Try again and the phone timer.

My recommendation is falling through to Ready. A free row needs nothing from the monitor to be rowable, so a failure card would block a row that would have worked.

What this does not change

  • No new colour, type size, or component. Every value is lifted from the two cards either side of it and from the workout's own programming card.
  • Cancel stays where it is, and still terminates the free row on the erg, which the walk on 3 September proved it does.
  • The ready card is untouched. It only arrives later, and by then it is true.
