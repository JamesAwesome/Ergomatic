## Method, and its reconciliation with the adversarial review

`reviewReplay()` re-runs the adversarial review's own instrument (its
B1: reachability capped at `after × 1.25`, upward only, Hall's condition
over the ordered bands, measured against the OLD warm-up-inclusive
grid). It reproduces the review's published numbers exactly:

| Hall deficit, O2 | 3 | 3 |
| Hall deficit, AT | 4 | 4 |

The solve then departs from that instrument in three spec-licensed ways
(module header, METHOD PROVENANCE): shrinking is legal, the one-rep arm
exists, and the 0/5 house rule discretises the scaling arm. It also
aims at §2's NEW draft grid, not the old one. Those four differences,
not an arithmetic disagreement, are why the replacement count below is
10 rather than the review's 21-at-exact-match / 8-at-±1.

**The ceiling's denominator** (review m7, left open by §3). Settled as
TOTAL time — the pieces scale and the rests scale with them, which is
§3's own "rest scaling in the same ratio family". Under the narrow
reading (rests held) the answer changes; both are priced below.

**The 0/5 totals rule.** Measured this session: it holds over PRE-STRIP
totals in 195 of 195 time-computable workouts and over post-strip

it forward (a retuned workout rounds its own warm-up-free total),
because the warm-up is a per-user setting now and no author controls
the pre-strip figure any more.
