What a baseline is, and why every pace comes from one ERGOMATIC · 3 MIN · UPDATED JUL 2026
A baseline is one honest piece you have actually rowed. Not a goal, not a test score you are proud of, just the pace you can hold over a known distance today.
IN THE APP 6K 2:02.4 → O2 = 6K −2 = 2:00.4 Every target you see carries the offset it came from, so you can always tell where a number was born.
Because targets are offsets, one new baseline moves your whole library at once. Row a faster 6k and the steady work follows it down without you editing a single workout. Keep separate baselines for the distances you actually row. A 2k and a 10k describe different rowers, and the app treats them that way.
