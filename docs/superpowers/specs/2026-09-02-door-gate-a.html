Gate 0-A · Wave F door spec §1–§3 · PR A

The saved row says what happened

A rower plans five 500s, stops after two and a bit, and the log shows a row with three dashes and no sentence. Nothing on that screen says the rower stopped — and nothing separates “I stopped” from “the app lost a boundary”. This gate is the one line that fixes it, plus the two words the log has been getting wrong (LOGGED BY HAND for a connected session that measured nothing, PM5 for an erg that never gave a name).

Tokens are app/src/theme/tokens.css verbatim; every frame class is copied from app/src/index.css by name with unchanged values. Frames are 372×640 portrait and 720×340 landscape at real proportions, and clip where a real phone clips. Fixture is the seeded library workout Tropical Wave (TR, 5 × 500 m at 2k + 2, 2:00 rest — server/seed/library/tr.ts), stopped mid-interval 3. Every ratio below is computed with the WCAG relative-luminance formula, not eyeballed. Nothing is implemented.

What I am asking you to approve

Five decisions. The frames below are the argument; these are the asks. Every word here is a draft you can reword — the shape is what needs your eyes.

(a) The marker words, one per close reason

One line beneath the meta, in the slot LINK LOST already uses. It renders only when the row is genuinely partial (connected, has a plan, at least one interval never measured, and a close reason that names who ended it).

LINK LOST keeps its existing sentence and its existing trigger untouched; it only gains the suffix. The other four words are new and render nowhere else.

(b) “Measured”, not “progress”

If a boundary is lost, a rower who did two and a bit reads 1 of 5 intervals measured. That is true of what the machine reported and silent about what was rowed. The alternative — counting what was rowed — is a number we do not have. Frame 2 shows it beside frame 1 so you approve that reading on sight.

(c) NO MONITOR READING, with a time

A connected session that arrived at the log with no record reads LOGGED BY HAND today — the same fact the live screen calls NO MONITOR READING. One fact, two words. It also gains the wall-clock time, because the app did witness the moment; by-hand rows stay time-free.

(d) MONITOR where PM5 was invented

An erg that advertises no name is stored as PM5 — a model number we do not know. The neutral fallback is the literal MONITOR, uppercase because TIMER and LOGGED BY HAND are and a real erg supplies its own caps. An erg that does name itself is unchanged.

(e) The chip in the list — which slot, and its short words

The list row gets a chip reusing .free-row-chip (the shipped JR chip) with no new colour. Two things need your word.

The slot. Section 7 renders both candidates. Beside the type badge, the chip eats the title — Tropical Wave becomes Tro…, measured, not guessed. On the numbers line it costs nothing, but Today’s LAST THREE rows render no numbers line, so they would carry no chip at all. My recommendation is the numbers line, accepting that Today shows no chip: Today is a three-row glance and the session is one tap from the sentence.

The chip border, honestly. --rule-3 on --page is 1.56:1. It is inherited unchanged from the shipped JR chip (.free-row-chip, index.css:548) and is decorative: the chip’s meaning is carried entirely by its text at 6.69:1, and removing the border would lose nothing an assistive reader relies on. If you want the outline to be a real boundary, --ink-4 #6f6a5f reaches 4.76:1 and would clear the 3:1 floor — but that would make the new chip louder than the shipped one, and the two would no longer match. Flagging it rather than silently deciding it.

Hit targets. The list row is the only tappable element these changes touch: .today-log-row, min-height: 46px, whole row, unchanged. The chip is not itself tappable. The detail screen’s marker is text, not a control. .back-link stays at min-height/min-width: 44px.

Where these frames are approximations

  • Fonts. Newsreader / Archivo / IBM Plex Mono load from Google Fonts, the same three families the app names in tokens.css. Rendering will differ by a hair from the phone’s own font stack.
  • Safe areas. .screen’s padding carries env(safe-area-inset-*) terms, which resolve to 0 in a desktop browser and to real values on a phone. The frames use the 0 case.
  • Judgment bars. Widths are the real formula (min(50, max(1.2, |dev|/1.6 × 50))): +2.0 s caps at 50%, −1.0 s is 31.25%.
  • The trace chart and plan footer are omitted below the fold in every frame; neither changes.
  • Numbers. The heroes for a stopped row are today’s FALLBACK tier (the stored, fused totals) — that is why TIME 7:45 sits beside AVG SPLIT 1:52.5: the time includes the programmed rest between intervals. Nothing in PR A changes those numbers.

This is the gate, not the presentation of a built thing. Nothing is implemented and no task starts until these five decisions have your word.
