3 · The not-advertising card (COPY) — the PM5 fact overturned the old line

James, at the erg: the PM5 advertises whenever it is awake and not already connected, on any screen, and an NFC tap wakes it. So "Open Connect Device on this PM5" asks for something the rower need not do. The states that remain are: asleep for longer than the scan (rare — the tap wakes it), or already connected to something else.
